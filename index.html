FAQ

Our licensed pharmacists are available to answer any

Are all Pharma-Deko products approved by NAFDAC?
Yes. All Pharma-Deko Plc products are fully registered and approved by NAFDAC before they reach the market. Every product carries a valid NAFDAC registration number printed clearly on its packaging.
Can individuals place orders directly on the website or is it for distributors only?
Individuals can place orders directly on our website. We serve both individual customers and distributors.
Can I order Pharma-Deko products if I am outside Nigeria?
Yes, we ship to select countries in West Africa. Contact our sales team for international orders.
What payment methods are accepted for online purchase; card, bank transfer, pay on delivery?
We accept card payments, bank transfers, and pay on delivery for eligible locations.
Where can I find the NAFDAC registration number for a specific product?
The NAFDAC registration number is printed on every product packaging and also listed on the product detail page.
How do I become a licensed Pharma-Deko distributor?
Contact our sales team through the Contact Us page to begin the distributor application process.
